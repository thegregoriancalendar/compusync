Connection help

Allow CompuSync through the firewall on your private network. Guest Wi-Fi may block laptop-to-laptop connections. If pairing fails, choose the Wi-Fi address above and copy a fresh invitation. A sleeping or fully quit app cannot receive a window; the connection returns automatically when it is available again.
